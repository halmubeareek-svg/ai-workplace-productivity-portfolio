# 🟡 Responsible AI Checklist — Business Consultant

## The Six Concepts, Applied to My Work

**Accuracy** — Is this actually true?
Before any client-facing figure or claim leaves my desk (like a renewal rate or a project date), I trace it back to a real source rather than trusting the AI output at face value — see the Verification Checklist in Section 5.

**Accountability** — Whose name is on this?
Every deliverable I send under my own name has been reviewed and approved by me first. AI drafts a starting point; I am responsible for the final version, not the tool.

**Confidentiality** — Could this leak something private?
I never paste unreleased client financials, internal pricing strategy, or proprietary engagement materials into a general-purpose AI tool unless it's an approved, firm-sanctioned platform with the right data-handling agreement in place.

**Privacy** — Does this involve a real person's data?
Client names, contact details, and any personally identifiable information are removed or replaced with placeholders (like "[Client Name]") in any example, template, or portfolio material I create or share.

**Bias** — Is this one-sided?
When I ask AI to summarize a client's concerns or a team's performance, I check that the summary fairly represents all sides — not just the loudest voice in the notes — and I ask follow-up questions if a summary reads one-sided.

**Human Oversight** — Did a person actually check this?
No AI-drafted output goes to a client or a partner without a human reading it first. This is the same rule as Step 4 of the Writing Workflow ("Human Sign-off") applied across every section of this portfolio.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification

Color key: `#1F8A4C` 🟢 Green — safe to share &nbsp;|&nbsp; `#E8A33D` 🟠 Amber — needs judgment/approval &nbsp;|&nbsp; `#C0392B` 🔴 Red — confidential, never share


Applying the classification framework to information actually used elsewhere in this portfolio:

| Information Item | Classification | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Generic client-timeline email template (Section 1) | 🟢 Green | No real client name, figures, or confidential details — safe to share and reuse as a template as-is. |
| Client call notes discussing pricing and the ~12% budget-overrun estimate (Section 3, checked in Section 5) | 🟠 Amber | Internal business/financial detail tied to an active engagement; requires judgment, verification, and partner approval before it goes anywhere near the client — exactly why it was run through the Verification Checklist first. |
| Real client identity — replaced everywhere in this portfolio with the placeholder "[Client Name]" (Sections 1, 2, 4, 7) | 🔴 Red | A real client's name and identifying details are confidential by default; that is precisely why every section uses a placeholder instead of an actual name. |

## Confirmation

No real confidential, private, or sensitive client or organizational information appears anywhere in this repository. Every name, figure, and scenario used across all seven sections is either fictional or a generic placeholder.
