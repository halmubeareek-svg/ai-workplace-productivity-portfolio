# 🟢 Professional Writing Example — Business Consultant

This example demonstrates the **4-Step Drafting Workflow** (Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off). I never send AI-generated writing as-is — every client-facing message passes through all four steps first.

**Scenario:** I need to tell the client's project sponsor that our data-cleanup workstream is delayed because a subcontractor missed a delivery.

## Step 1 — AI-Generated Draft

> Prompt used: *"Write a quick update about the project delay."*
>
> Draft: "Hi team, I wanted to give you a quick update. There's been a delay on the project due to some issues with a vendor. We're working on it and will keep you posted. Thanks."

This first draft is fast but generic — no specific cause, no new date, no ownership of next steps.

## Step 2 — Fact Verification

I checked the draft against what I actually know about the situation:
- ✅ Confirmed: the delay is caused by the subcontractor missing the **May 15** data-delivery date.
- ✅ Confirmed: the new internal target is **May 29**.
- ⚠️ Corrected: the draft didn't mention that this only affects the data-cleanup workstream — the analysis workstream is unaffected. Added this in.
- ⚠️ Flagged: I do not yet have a confirmed replacement date from the subcontractor, so I marked this as [Not Specified] rather than guessing.

## Step 3 — Tone Refinement

- Removed casual phrasing ("just wanted to give you a quick update") — this goes to a client sponsor, not an internal team.
- Made the tone calm, direct, and solution-focused rather than apologetic.
- Added one clear next step so the sponsor isn't left wondering what happens next.

## Step 4 — Human Review / Sign-off

I read the final version once more as if I were the sponsor receiving it, confirmed the dates were correct against the project tracker, and approved it before sending under my own name.

## Final Version

> Subject: Data-Cleanup Workstream — Short Delay, New Target Date
>
> Hi [Sponsor Name],
>
> I want to flag a short delay on the data-cleanup workstream: our subcontractor missed the May 15 delivery of the source files. The analysis workstream is unaffected and remains on schedule.
>
> Our new internal target for data-cleanup is May 29. I'm following up with the subcontractor today to confirm a firm replacement date and will update you by end of week.
>
> Happy to jump on a call if useful in the meantime.
>
> Best,
> [Consultant Name] *(Human-approved and sent under my name.)*
