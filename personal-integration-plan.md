# 🔵 Personal Integration Plan — Business Consultant

Three recurring tasks in my role where I plan to keep using generative AI, following the four-step Writing Workflow and the Verification Protocol built during this course.

## Task 1 — Weekly Client Status Emails

- **Expected benefit:** Cut drafting time roughly in half by starting from a structured, few-shot prompt instead of writing each update from scratch.
- **Preferred prompt/workflow:** The "Weekly Client Status Email" prompt from my Prompt Library (Section 1), followed by the full 4-Step Drafting Workflow before sending.
- **Risk / verification requirement:** Every date, figure, and commitment mentioned must be checked against the project tracker before the email goes to the client (Verification Protocol, Section 5).
- **How often I'll use it:** Weekly, every Friday.
- **What success looks like:** Client status emails go out on time every week with zero factual corrections needed after sending.

## Task 2 — Turning Meeting Notes into Executive Briefs

- **Expected benefit:** Faster turnaround from a messy call or meeting to a decision-ready brief a partner can act on immediately, instead of a flat retelling of the notes.
- **Preferred prompt/workflow:** The "Decision-Ready Executive Brief" C.A.R.E. prompt from Section 1, applied using the Information Workflow shown in Section 3 (raw notes → prompt → AI output → human review → final brief).
- **Risk / verification requirement:** Never let the AI fill in an owner, deadline, or decision that wasn't actually stated — always mark it [Not Specified] and follow up, per the Golden Rule.
- **How often I'll use it:** After every significant client call, roughly 2–3 times per week.
- **What success looks like:** Partners can act on a brief without needing to go back to the raw notes themselves.

## Task 3 — Structuring New Engagement or Service Plans

- **Expected benefit:** Move from a vague goal to an assignable, phased plan (with owners, deadlines, and risks) in under an hour instead of a full afternoon.
- **Preferred prompt/workflow:** The structured C.A.R.E. planning prompt and Planning Chain from Section 4 (Goal → Mechanisms → Phases → Tasks, then Dependencies → Owners → Deadlines → Risks).
- **Risk / verification requirement:** Confirm the plan's constraints (team size, timeframe, budget) are accurate before sharing, and have a senior consultant sanity-check the risk list before the plan is finalized.
- **How often I'll use it:** At the start of every new engagement or internal initiative, roughly monthly.
- **What success looks like:** New engagement plans are ready to review within the same day the goal is set, with every task assigned to a named owner.
